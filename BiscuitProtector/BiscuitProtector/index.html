📖 The Official Dipping Handbook

Welcome to Biscuit Protector! The golden rule of tea time: a biscuit dipped too short lacks flavor; a biscuit dipped too long sinks into eternity.

🎮 How to Play

  1. Select your biscuit & beverage: Higher density biscuits (like Rusk or Digestive) hold up longer, while light biscuits (Parle-G) crumble fast! Hotter beverages accelerate soggy absorption.
  2. Press START DIP (or Spacebar): Watch the liquid soak into the biscuit and listen to the ticking tension audio.
  3. Press SAVE BISCUIT (or Spacebar): Click just before the soggy meter hits 100%!
  4. Avoid the Drop: If the timer exceeds 100%, your biscuit will snap and fall to the bottom of the cup with a sad plop!

🔥 Game Modes

  • Classic Dip: Practice precision timing. Target 100/100 points by stopping right on the target time.
  • Endurance Challenge: Survive continuous rounds of random biscuits. One lost biscuit ends your streak!
  • Zen Practice: Free dipping with visual soak indicators without game overs.
